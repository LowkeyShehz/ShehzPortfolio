Shehz Studios

An AI-native indie game studio. We make stylized, low poly multiplayer games, and use AI to let a very small team ship what used to take a very large one.

First Title Concept

Multiplayer Zombie Apocalypse

Working title · stylized low poly · multiplayer

Our first game drops players into a stylized, low poly zombie apocalypse and asks them to survive it together. The low poly look is a deliberate choice: it gives the game a strong, readable identity and keeps art production fast enough for a small team to iterate on, while staying light enough to run on a wide range of devices.

Unity C# Low Poly Art Online Multiplayer

Roadmap

  1. Concept & core loop

    Defining the survival loop, art direction and multiplayer design.

  2. Playable prototype

    Movement, combat and zombie AI in a small test map.

  3. Multiplayer playtests

    Networked sessions with real players and feedback loops.

How we build with AI

AI-assisted engineering

We use Claude to write, review and refactor gameplay code, tooling and systems in C#. Every change is reviewed and tested by a human developer, so AI speeds up the work without lowering the bar.

AI-accelerated art pipeline

A low poly style suits AI-assisted asset workflows: faster concepting, quicker iteration on props and environments, and a consistent look. Art direction and final polish stay in human hands.

Small team, proven builder

The founder has already built a Unity action RPG prototype with enemy AI and NavMesh, an AR history app, and Flutter and Next.js products (see below). The studio grows out of that hands-on experience.
